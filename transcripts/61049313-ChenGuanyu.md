I agreed with C's objection and opened a separate OpenCode follow-up session scoped strictly to `test_accept_append_open_failure_raises_and_keeps_old_bytes`. I directed the agent to remove `_block_append` from this test (keeping the shared helper intact for the rejection and call-log tests), require exactly one targeted `mkstemp` hit including the `.tmp` suffix, and assert that no temporary-file residue remained in the ledger directory.
